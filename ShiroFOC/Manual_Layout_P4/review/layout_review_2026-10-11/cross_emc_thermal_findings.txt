P4 targeted cross-domain, EMC and thermal review — 2026-10-11

Scope and evidence
Read-only review of P4 source; cross_analysis.json, thermal.json and emc.json
were generated using schematic.json and pcb_refilled.json. Geometry checks in
targeted_geometry_audit.json independently parse the assessment PCB and include
both standalone vias and footprint-embedded plated pads. No layout was changed.

Actionable remaining layout work
1. U4 exposed-pad ground connection is unfinished [deterministic / geometry].
   EP65 is 4.0894 x 4.0894 mm, centred at (-5.1, 7.2). There are zero GND vias
   within its pad and no embedded plated pads in U4. The nearest plated GND
   connection is a standalone via 4.936 mm from the pad centre. Add the intended
   EP ground/thermal via arrangement and copper return before routing signoff;
   select actual via geometry against the package land pattern and assembly
   process. Do not quote an arbitrary minimum count from this analyzer.

2. Finish short local protection returns [deterministic geometry; EMC impact
   is engineering judgement]. D801 CAN TVS GND3 at (-18.75, 2.785) has its
   nearest plated GND connection 5.452 mm away. U202 USB protector GND2 at
   (-14.76, -6.0825) has its nearest dedicated GND via 4.614 mm away; a USB
   shield plated hole is closer at 2.965 mm. Put a short direct low-inductance
   ground connection beside each protection device as routing is completed.
   D601 already has one GND via 0.067 mm from its GND-pad centre; treat it
   separately, rather than claiming all protection devices lack ground vias.

3. Complete local supply decoupling at the feedback interface [deterministic
   topology/geometry; capacitor requirements should be confirmed against the
   actual devices]. No adjacent 3V3-to-GND capacitor is placed at U602 VDD16
   or U603 VCC8. The nearest appropriate capacitor power pad found is C802,
   approximately 18.56 mm and 11.24 mm away respectively. Place/connect local
   supply decouplers and local GND returns. The EMC U602 finding incorrectly
   selected the VBUS_SENSE filter as its supply capacitor.

4. Finish the motor-power copper and verify losses under specified operating
   conditions [deterministic topology; ratings not assessed]. Only GND zones
   exist. VM has only 3.677 mm of track; PHASE_U/V/W have no track segments.
   Low-side source paths each have one 2.31 mm segment. The high-current bus,
   phases, shunts, brake path, capacitor loops and heat-spreading copper need
   completion. Q501 has no drain thermal vias or finished heat-spreading pour.
   No safe current rating is inferred from these incomplete traces.

5. Preserve the explicit high-side gate-return pairing [topology plus layout
   judgement]. GHS1/2/3 and HS_RETURN_U/V/W each transition between F.Cu and
   B.Cu using two vias. Finish/verify compact gate-to-source loops and keep
   the return accompanying the gate. The generic recommendation to add GND
   beside every high-side gate transition is not the relevant return path.
   Route shunt sense pairs as Kelvin connections with low pickup, away from
   switching copper; generic high-speed differential skew limits do not apply.

6. Schematic/PCB sync [deterministic topology, parent intent triage]. Cross
   analysis lists R618 plus six DNP components as missing. R618 is the fitted
   U604 current-limit resistor and must be resolved. The six intentional DNP
   omissions are not equivalent fitted-component failures.

Raw analyzer results and false-positive triage
- Cross-domain: 6 findings; EMC: 86 findings (19 errors, 50 warnings, 17 info).
  These raw counts/scores are not a prioritized defect count or readiness score.
- Cross PS-002 counts disconnected same-net copper/pads as "plane islands";
  it must not be presented as 199 fragmented GND pours. Refilled GND zones each
  have one fill region. Many pads still require routing.
- GP-004's 26% fill ratio uses the obsolete 79 mm zone-outline area as its
  denominator. Refilled copper is 1,630.06 mm2 per GND layer, approximately
  80.5% of the 45 x 45 mm bounding square, not 26% of the current board.
- SU-001 treats all KiCad layer types "signal" literally, ignoring the actual
  In1/In4 ground planes and layer intent; its adjacent-signal errors are false.
- GLS3 has an off-board leftover via at (45.56, 49.59); do not infer a real
  signal-over-plane-gap defect from its 83% reference-coverage score alone.
- NRST is misclassified as a clock from its sheet path. U202 USB ESD IC is
  misclassified as a connector. J1 motor phases are not ordinary digital I/O.
- The shunt-sense 50 ps length-matching error is inapplicable; avoid adding
  serpentine length solely to satisfy that heuristic.
- SW-003 chooses off-board C150 for the U201 hot loop. Verify the actual local
  C201/C202 input-cap loop after placement/routing; do not use its 64 mm2 claim.
- Several decoupling-via measurements ignore net identity or embedded pads.
- The thermal-pad analyzer's strict via-count loop counts unrelated nets.
  Its U4 count of six vias is wrong; the six include signal vias. Its separate
  thermal_pad_scan count of one GND via is a wide-radius nearby count, not an
  in-pad count. The direct geometry audit above supersedes both for U4.

Thermal and simulation limits
thermal.json reports zero warnings but assessed only U201, using an assumed
25 mA load, guessed 85% efficiency, unknown-package default thermal resistance,
and 25 C ambient. U4 was skipped and the motor bridges were not modeled.
Its temperature/100-point score cannot support thermal or current signoff.
Need intended bus range, continuous/peak phase current, switching frequency,
brake duty, enclosure/cooling, copper weight and actual dissipation assumptions.

LTspice.app is installed. The SPICE skill attempted 35 isolated RC/divider
testbenches, including a second attempt using absolute paths. Every simulation
was skipped/failed with no useful measurements or diagnostic output. spice.json
and spice_work preserve the attempts. No SPICE verification is claimed; motor
bridge switching behavior and regulator stability were not simulated.

EMC analysis was analytical only, with unfinished routing and heuristic device
classification. It does not establish emissions compliance. When routing is
complete, reassess local power loops, gate/return loops, shunt pickup, connector
returns and decoupling, then perform appropriate measurements on hardware.
