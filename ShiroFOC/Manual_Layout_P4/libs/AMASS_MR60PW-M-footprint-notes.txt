AMASS MR60PW-M footprint
=======================

Footprint: ShiroFOCLib:AMASS_MR60PW-M_1x03_P5.50mm_Horizontal
Source: AMASS product specification 2023V2, PCB LAYOUT drawing.
https://www.china-amass.com/public/upload/20240530/b96cfb0ffabb422135a2a15093576222.pdf

Manufacturer-dimensioned features, in mm:
  Contact pitch: 5.50 +/-0.05
  Finished contact holes: diameter 2.90 +0.10/-0.00
  Two soldered mounting tabs: plated slots 0.80 x 1.90, both +0.10/-0.00
  Mounting slot center spacing: 17.10 +/-0.05
  Mounting slot row offset from contact row: 6.00 +/-0.05
  Body: 19.20 wide x 19.50 deep x 8.20 high

Footprint conventions:
  Contact 1 is the origin; contacts 2 and 3 are at X=5.50 and X=11.00.
  Mating direction is negative Y on the front of the PCB.
  Slot centers: (-3.05, -6.00), (14.05, -6.00).
  Mounting tabs are unnumbered plated pads and carry no phase net.
  Contact copper diameter/width is 4.20; mounting copper is 1.80 x 2.90.
  Body rear edge is at Y=2.10, inferred from the outline drawing and an
  existing community footprint; that offset is not explicitly dimensioned
  by AMASS. The drilled hole/slot pattern uses the explicit AMASS dimensions.
  Courtyard surrounds the connector body and pads with at least 0.50 clearance.
  The mating plug and cable access envelope is not part of the courtyard.
  No 3D model is assigned.

Project electrical assignment: J1 pin 1 = PHASE_U, 2 = PHASE_V, 3 = PHASE_W.
AMASS does not number the three contacts in the cited drawing. The project
assignment must also be used for the mating motor harness.

This is a project-local footprint, not a bundled KiCad library footprint.
